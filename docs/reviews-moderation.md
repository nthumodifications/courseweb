# Native course review moderation

Course reviews are student-authored opinions about a course and instructor. Staff may remove reviews that contain personal attacks, leaked exam content, doxxing or other identifying private information, or spam. A negative but honest opinion is not by itself a moderation reason.

Three distinct open reports automatically hide a review until a moderator restores, hides, or removes it. Moderators should record a reason for every decision and may dismiss reports that do not support moderation.

Author identity is visible only to staff in the moderation queue. Public review responses never expose the author's user ID or name.
